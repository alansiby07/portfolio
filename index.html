Research

1-Axis Spacecraft Attitude Control

Institute for Computing in Research Summer 2025
As a research intern at the Institute for Computing in Research, I built a Python simulation of a spacecraft attitude control system. The model used PD control to stabilize orientation and accounted for reaction wheel saturation, a real limitation that affects how spacecraft respond to commands. I ran a grid search to optimize the controller's gains, then co-authored and published the findings on engrXiv.
Tools & Skills
Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, control theory, simulation, optimization
Outcome
Published research paper on engrXiv with full technical documentation

Product Redesign

Magic Snap: Ergonomic Mouse Redesign

Engineering Design and Development 2026
Working with my team, Flow State, I led a full ergonomic redesign of the Apple Magic Mouse. The original design causes wrist strain, has too much surface friction, and requires an awkward charging position that makes the mouse unusable while plugged in. Our redesign is modular and 3D printable, with a magnetic charging system that solves the charging problem without sacrificing comfort. We validated the design through multiple rounds of CAD iteration and hands on user testing.
Tools
Autodesk Inventor, 3D printing, user testing
Skills
Human factors, CAD design, prototyping, iteration
Outcome
Functional prototype with validated ergonomic design, open source model
Team
Flow State (Ashmeet Bedi, Niketh Raghu)

Reverse Engineering

Hair Dryer Teardown and Performance Testing

Product Dissection / Testing 2025
I disassembled a hair dryer to build a functional model that mapped how each internal component contributed to the device's overall performance. After documenting the teardown, I ran physical tests measuring heat output, airspeed, noise level, and dry time across both heat settings, then compared the results to draw conclusions about the tradeoffs in the design.
Tools
Thermometer, anemometer, decibel meter, hand tools
Skills
Reverse engineering, experimental design, data analysis, technical reporting
Deliverable
Functional model diagram, four trial performance data, presentation slides
Data Points
4 performance metrics (heat, airspeed, noise, dry time) averaged across 2 settings

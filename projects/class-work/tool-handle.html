Ergonomic Tool Handle Redesign

Collaborated with a local tool manufacturer to redesign a hand tool handle using biomechanical principles, resulting in injection-molding-ready geometry with full DFM documentation.

Category Capstone Project — Industry Partner
Year 2023
Tools SolidWorks, 3D Printing
Add photos or diagrams here

Overview

Our capstone team partnered with a local Austin tool company to redesign the handle of a professional-grade screwdriver. Their customer feedback highlighted grip fatigue during extended use. My role covered ergonomic analysis, CAD modeling, and DFM review.

Ergonomic Research

I reviewed grip force literature and OSHA hand tool guidelines to establish design targets: maximize contact area, avoid pressure concentration at the palm, and match the natural hand curvature. We also conducted informal user testing with foam mockups.

CAD & Surfacing

The revised handle was modeled in SolidWorks using lofted and boundary surfaces to achieve smooth organic geometry. Key features included a palm swell for power grip, a finger-indent zone for precision grip, and a textured TPE overmold region.

DFM Review

Working with the manufacturer's tooling team, I revised the model to eliminate undercuts, ensure uniform wall thickness (3mm nominal), and add appropriate draft angles (1.5° minimum). A full DFM report was submitted alongside the final CAD package.

Outcome

The manufacturer approved the design for tooling development. Physical prototypes were 3D printed in Shore 60A TPU to simulate the overmold and validated with a small user test group, who rated grip comfort 40% higher than the original.

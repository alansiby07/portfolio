Structural Analysis

Lightweight Drone Frame

Applied topology optimization to a UAV airframe to achieve a 35% weight reduction while maintaining all required structural safety factors under flight load conditions.

Category Senior Design — Team Project
Year 2024
Tools ANSYS Mechanical, Fusion 360
Add photos or diagrams here

Overview

As part of our senior design capstone, my team was tasked with redesigning the main frame of a fixed-wing UAV for an educational drone platform. My role was structural lead — responsible for load case definition, FEA, and the topology optimization workflow.

Load Cases

We identified three critical load cases: max-g pull-up maneuver (3g), hard landing impact (5g vertical), and asymmetric gust loading. Each was translated into equivalent static loads applied at motor mounts and landing gear attachment points.

Topology Optimization

Using ANSYS Mechanical's topology optimization module, I set up the design domain with non-design regions at all attachment points. The optimizer was run with a 50% volume fraction target and minimum member size constraint to ensure printability.

Validation

The optimized geometry was re-meshed and run through a full FEA validation pass. All safety factors exceeded 2.0 under the defined load cases. Final mass came in at 312g versus the original 480g — a 35% reduction.

Manufacturing

The optimized frame was 3D printed in PETG for proof of concept, then a final version was cut from 3mm carbon fiber sheet using a waterjet cutter. Physical load testing confirmed the simulation predictions within 8%.

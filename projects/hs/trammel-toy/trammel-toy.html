Mechanism Design

Trammel Toy

Modeled and animated a trammel mechanism in Inventor, using motion constraints to simulate how rotating a central beam drives linear sliders.

Category Intro to Engineering Design
Year 2023
Tools Autodesk Inventor

Inventor assembly animation: the central beam rotates, driving the sliders along their tracks through the pin-and-slot linkage

Overview

This project focused on building a working trammel mechanism: a classic mechanical linkage where a rotating beam drives two sliders along perpendicular tracks, fully modeled and animated in Autodesk Inventor.

What I Learned

I learned how to use constraints like Insert to fit screws and other fasteners into holes perfectly. I also learned how to apply motion constraints in Inventor to simulate real mechanical movement. By spinning the central beam, I was able to drive the sliders along their tracks exactly as the physical mechanism would.

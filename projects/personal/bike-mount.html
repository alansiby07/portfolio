Parametric Bike Mount System

Designed a fully parametric handlebar mount system published on Printables, downloaded 800+ times, using print orientation analysis to maximize layer-line strength.

Category Personal Project — Published Design
Year 2022
Tools SolidWorks, FDM Printer (PETG)
Add photos or diagrams here

Overview

I needed a clean way to mount my phone and bike computer to my handlebars without buying an expensive proprietary system. I designed a modular parametric mount system in SolidWorks and published it for free — it ended up getting a lot more downloads than I expected.

Parametric Design

The mount uses a SolidWorks design table to drive all key dimensions from three inputs: handlebar diameter, device width, and clamp thickness. This lets anyone generate a correctly-sized version without editing the model manually.

Print Orientation Analysis

The clamp arm is the highest-stress part — it's loaded in bending when you hit a bump. I analyzed three print orientations and chose the one that puts layer lines parallel to the bending stress direction, which increased the effective flexural strength by about 60% vs. the weakest orientation.

Material Choice

I chose PETG over PLA for UV and heat resistance — a phone mount left in the sun on a summer day can easily exceed PLA's heat deflection temperature. The final design uses 4 perimeters and 40% gyroid infill for a good stiffness-to-weight ratio.

Community Response

The design was featured in Printables' weekly newsletter and reached 800+ downloads. Several users messaged to say they'd adapted it for motorcycle handlebars and scooters, which was cool to see.
